Privacy policy

01General

This Policy sets out how ООО «Власта-Консалтинг» (the Company) processes personal data and the measures taken to keep it secure.

By using the site and contacting us by phone or email, you accept the terms of this Policy.

02What we process

Your name, company, phone number, email address and the content of your enquiry — only as far as you provide it yourself.

Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, referral source and on-site activity, in anonymised form, for statistics.

03Purposes

Responding to your enquiry and providing a consultation.

Improving the site and the quality of our services.

Meeting the requirements of the law of the Russian Federation.

04Legal basis

Processing is carried out on the basis of your consent and in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data”.

05Disclosure to third parties

The Company does not sell or pass personal data to third parties, except where the law expressly requires it or where it is necessary to act on your enquiry.

06Retention

Personal data is kept no longer than the purposes of processing require, or until you withdraw your consent.

07Cookies

The site uses cookies so the interface works correctly and to collect anonymised statistics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

08Your rights

You may request information about the processing of your data, ask for it to be corrected, blocked or deleted, and withdraw your consent to processing.

To exercise these rights, write to info@vlasta-s.com.
