Our story

Experience that earns trust

Founded in 2006, Vlasta Consulting holds a leading position in the business security field. Over the years we have built deep expertise in intellectual property protection and anti-counterfeiting, as well as in corporate and personal security.

Our methodology is built on an integrated approach: rather than merely reacting to threats, we analyse risks and anticipate adverse scenarios in advance. This lets us prevent problems before they damage the client's business.

The company is a member of international professional associations (including the World Association of Detectives, WAD) and represents the interests of multinational corporations in Russia and the CIS in partnership with firms abroad.
