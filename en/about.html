Company history

A record marked byinternational recognition

  1. 2006

    The company is founded; operations begin in Moscow in business security and brand protection. Development of law-enforcement practice under the “counterfeit” articles of the Russian Code of Administrative Offences and Criminal Code.

  2. 2010

    More than 10 successful brand-protection programmes and further development of the practice of shutting down illegal production. CEO Vladimir Solomanidin is elected head of the World Association of Detectives (WAD, founded in 1925).

  3. 2014–15

    The decade’s largest raid on illegal production — seizure of more than 100 truckloads of finished counterfeit goods. The company team exceeds 50 people. Company leadership is recognised with two awards at once (the Norman J. Sloan Award from WAD, and the V.V. Leontief medal “For Achievements in Economics”).

  4. 2020

    Reaching a rate of 15 illegal production sites closed per year. The company portfolio exceeds 80 brands; active work begins against illicit excise goods. An online practice across a number of foreign jurisdictions.

  5. 2022

    A contribution pivotal for Russia’s law-enforcement system to sustaining market protection against illicit goods under sanctions pressure. Alexey Solomanidin becomes the youngest president in WAD history.

  6. 2024

    Active development of practices against threats in the online environment and against unfair competition. Co-authorship of a number of specialist initiatives and legislative amendments.

  7. 2025

    A return to “peak” performance; the geography of operations expands to the Eurasian Economic Union countries with the launch of comprehensive brand-protection programmes on new markets. Prevented damage for rights holders — more than €125,000,000 a year. CEO Vladimir Solomanidin is awarded a medal by the All-Russian Police Association (MPA).
