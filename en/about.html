  1. 2006

    The company is founded

  2. 2010

    International recognition

  3. 2014

    The Norman J. Sloan Award

    A commemorative award presented by the Association of Detectives to Vladimir Solomanidin for long and distinguished service in investigations and security.

  4. 2015

    The V.V. Leontief medal

    Vladimir Solomanidin awarded the medal “For Achievements in Economics”.
