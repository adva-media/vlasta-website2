Shutting down counterfeit distribution channels

A combined online and offline programme covering the whole of Russia: a zero-tolerance strategy cut the number of retail outlets carrying counterfeit by 85%.

PracticeBrand protection
RegionRussia · CIS · EAEU
Outcome−85% outlets carrying fakes

Within six months the number of outlets found carrying counterfeit fell by 85%, and no further shipments entered the country.

01Situation

A trial wholesale consignment of counterfeit goods was brought into Russia.

To counter it, a combined online and offline programme covering the whole territory of Russia was agreed with the client.

02Problem

The infringers chose small retail as their sales channel, as the safest option for them. Wholesalers did not sell directly, which also shielded them from raids.

That left blocking the final links in the chain and working with small volumes. Given the number of retail outlets across the country, the damage to the business could have been enormous.

03Solution

We applied a zero-tolerance strategy to counterfeit both offline and online. Infringements were monitored across Russia, and every confirmed case — even one holding only a couple of counterfeit units — was passed to law enforcement. Online, we covered not only pure e-commerce but classified-ad boards as well.

In parallel we found listings offering large wholesale lots (several pallets). Work on those ended in raids, seizure of the counterfeit goods and criminal proceedings.

04Outcome

After six months of sustained work the number of outlets found carrying counterfeit fell by 85%, and stock on hand dropped to 8–10 units. With infringing listings closed as fast as they appeared, new ones became rare.

The infringers explained that retail had stopped taking their goods because of the pressure on the market; that blocked listings left them unable to move volume through e-commerce; and that with their channels closed they finally decided to dump the consignment wholesale at knock-down prices simply to recover costs.

After that series of raids no further counterfeit shipments entered the country, and attention moved to other product lines of the client.
