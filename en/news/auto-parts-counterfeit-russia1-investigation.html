40% of Counterfeit Parts in the Auto Market: How to Avoid Fakes and Prevent Double Spending

A recent investigation by Russia 1 TV revealed that up to 40% of auto parts and motor oils in Russia are counterfeit. Drivers trying to save money end up with fakes—leading to breakdowns, costly repairs, and forced purchases of genuine parts.

Shocking Stats: Every Second Part Could Be Fake

A recent investigation by Russia 1 TV revealed that up to 40% of auto parts and motor oils in Russia are counterfeit. Drivers trying to save money end up with fakes—leading to breakdowns, costly repairs, and forced purchases of genuine parts. The result? Double spending and safety risks.

The report emphasized labeling as a way to spot fakes. But the problem runs deeper.

Why Is There So Much Counterfeit Product on the Market?

Experts from Russia 1 TV highlight two major challenges:

  • Mixed Shipments: Fakes Hidden Among Genuine Parts

Even trusted suppliers might unknowingly deliver batches contaminated with counterfeits. Blind trust isn’t enough—every part must be checked.

  • Marketplaces vs. Repair Shops: Where’s the Risk Higher?

Professional auto services inspect every component before installation. Meanwhile, major online platforms often fail to vet sellers, allowing fakes to be sold as branded goods.

What Does Vlasta-Consulting Propose?

Fighting counterfeits requires more than consumer awareness—it demands market reforms. The solution is a systematic approach.

1.Support for Legal Distributors – Strengthening verification mechanisms, warehouse controls, and supply chain oversight.

2.Cooperation with Regulators – Tighter controls, swift confiscation of counterfeit goods, and suppression of illegal trade.

3.Transparent Supply Chains – From manufacturer to end consumer.

Vlasta-Consulting is committed to making the auto parts market safer.
