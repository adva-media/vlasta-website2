Brand protection & IP

Law enforcement agencies, together with specialists of Vlasta-Consulting company suppressed the activities of a counterfeit shoe factory in the Vladimir region

In November 2023 a large-scale operation to suppress the illegal activity of the illicit production facility in Vladimir Oblast was conducted by the officers from the Main Department of Economic Security and Anti-Corruption of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Russia.

The infringers were manufacturing counterfeit sneakers under several popular brands hiding behind the production of their own goods. In the frames of the raid action about 640 000 pairs of sneakers were revealed and seized by LEA officers.

The criminal cases for the illegal usage of means of individualization of goods (art. 180 part 3 of the Criminal Code) were initiated by the Investigation Department of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of the city of Gus’-Khrustal’ny.
