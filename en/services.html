Brand and IP protection

Trademark registration

A trademark is both the face of a business and an asset on its balance sheet. Before it can be used it needs legal protection: we take a mark from the initial clearance search through to the certificate and beyond, in Russia and abroad.

  • Clearance search and filing
  • Prosecution through to the certificate
  • Renewals, amendments, foreign registration
  • Licensing and third-party agreements
Full-scope trademark defence

Defence works when it is systematic and covers every type of infringement at once. Using the full set of instruments together is what produces the highest level of protection a mark can have.

  • Identity and confusing similarity
  • Unfair competition
  • Online and offline run as one loop
  • Monitoring for repeat offences afterwards
Online Brand Protection System

Our own web application finds infringing content of any kind and handles the pre-action work with site administrators, hosting providers and domain registrars.

  • Digital fingerprint matching (hash comparison)
  • Linked to our VCC system: physical addresses for raids
  • Profiling of administrators and affiliated resources
  • Automatic re-infringement monitoring
Pre-action measures online

Most online infringement comes down without litigation, provided you move quickly and address every party at once.

  • Suspicious links pre-selected by our analysts
  • Complaints to the administrator, registrar and host
  • Take-down tools for rapid delisting on major platforms
  • Blocking of advertising and search results
Offline brand protection

An online trail leads to a physical address. From there it is a test purchase, an inspection and a raid alongside law enforcement.

  • Test purchase and examination of the goods offered
  • Establishing the real address behind the offer
  • Inspections carried out with police officers
  • Case support in court and supervision of destruction
Civil claims

Where a rights holder would rather act without involving law enforcement, a civil claim is often the most effective route — and it complements administrative and criminal measures well.

  • Recovery of damages and statutory compensation
  • Seizure and destruction of counterfeit at the infringer’s cost
  • Cancellation of third-party marks that block your business
  • Injunctions against use of a mark or copyrighted work

Raids with the police

Raids on production sites, retail outlets and warehouses are among the most effective instruments in the programme. Our work with law enforcement is set up at every stage, from spotting the infringement to destroying the goods.

  1. 01

    Monitoring and intelligence

    Our own investigation before any application to the police: who is infringing, where the shops and warehouses are, and how the goods move.

  2. 02

    Organising and joining the raid

    Our specialists work the raid alongside the police and provide expert support, so the fakes are seized and the infringer is held to account.

  3. 03

    Independent examination of seized goods

    Selecting the expert body and running the examinations that establish the goods are counterfeit and the rights were infringed.

  4. 04

    Legal support

    Conducting the criminal and administrative cases opened after the raid, so that the infringers do not slip away from liability.

  5. 05

    Destruction of the counterfeit

    Supervising destruction once the court has ruled: the fakes must not find their way back onto the market.

  6. 06

    Post-raid monitoring

    Regular checks catch repeat offences and stop production or sales from starting up again.

Customs and the IP register

Working with the Russian Federal Customs Service and with customs authorities abroad stops counterfeit goods before they ever reach the market.

  1. 01

    Protection strategy

    First we establish the main routes and methods by which counterfeit enters the country, then we set the order of action.

  2. 02

    Entry into the Russian customs register

    Registering marks and copyrighted works in the Russian customs register. We have entered dozens of objects into it.

  3. 03

    Foreign customs registers

    If the goods come in through Belarus or Kazakhstan, the mark is entered into the national registers of those EAEU states.

  4. 04

    Acting for the rights holder

    Responding to customs alerts and enquiries, supplying documents, and supporting the administrative cases in court.

  5. 05

    Customs seminars and training

    Keeping officers current on the signs of counterfeiting, running training seminars, giving legal guidance, joint projects.

  6. 06

    Post-clearance control

    Customs may also act on goods already inside the Customs Union: we provide the expert and legal support for those raids.

  7. 07

    Action against parallel imports

    Original goods imported without the brand owner’s consent are stopped by the register and a claim for compensation.

Litigation

Our lawyers run cases in the commercial courts and the courts of general jurisdiction, from first instance through to enforcement.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    Reading the conflict and choosing the strategy, pre-action or litigation, with most of the risks weighed at the planning stage.

  2. 02

    Cease and desist letters

    A warning letter demanding the infringement stops is the quickest and cheapest way to close a dispute.

  3. 03

    Preparing and filing the claim

    Gathering and analysing evidence, drafting the statement of claim and running the case through to judgment.

  4. 04

    Appeals

    Acting for the rights holder when decisions are appealed to the higher courts.

  5. 05

    Enforcement

    Supporting enforcement proceedings, both for payment of compensation and for the remaining relief.

2

Business security

Compliance and counterparty screening

We know the risks foreign companies meet in Russia, the CIS and Eastern Europe, and we prepare screening to American and European standards.

  • KYC / AML conformity
  • Full due diligence to FCPA / UKBA
  • Identification of politically exposed persons
  • Sanctions risk assessment
Background screening

However spotless a CV looks, a new relationship can still carry real risk. Screening closes off conflicts of interest, criminal ties and hidden episodes in a history.

  • Reputational material in open sources
  • Registration data and business connections
  • Wanted-persons databases and sanctions lists
  • Regulator registers and court records
  • Source enquiries and interviews
Online candidate screening system

A web application processes the questionnaire automatically and places the candidate on a risk level, with no separate in-house vetting team and no months of waiting.

  • The candidate fills in the form himself, using a personal token
  • The system compiles the primary data automatically
  • The deeper analysis sits with our specialists
  • The result: a risk level and a report on the candidate
Corporate investigations

Suspicions of fraud, embezzlement or collusion are rarely something a company can unpick from the inside. The schemes we have uncovered had cost clients profit measured in billions of roubles.

  • Investigation of fraud
  • Detection of asset misappropriation
  • Breaches of business ethics
  • Due diligence in mergers and acquisitions
Corporate disputes

Threats to corporate governance come from outside and from within, particularly where unscrupulous players are used to working around the law and business ethics.

  • Countering blackmail and extortion
  • Shutting down defamation campaigns
  • Precautions against greenmail
  • Countering manipulation of financial reporting
Close protection

Bodyguards with an analytical turn of mind, English, and an appearance that draws no attention. Armed or unarmed, according to the risk profile.

  • Protection for principals and company guests
  • The detail is sized to the risk profile
  • English-speaking coordinators on call 24/7
Transport security

From Meet & Greet through to details running several days. The class of vehicle and the driver’s training are matched to the assignment.

  • A premium saloon for the principal, escort SUVs alongside
  • A Mercedes-Benz V-class shuttle for groups
  • Armoured vehicles and drivers trained in evasive driving
Event security

At events covered by the press, even a minor incident is a reputational risk for the organisers. This is where a seamless security perimeter earns its place.

  • Screening of the guest and participant lists
  • Venue assessment, evacuation routes, response to incidents
  • Travel logistics, passes, coordination of contractors
  • Security at places of residence, hotels included
An integrated security system

The further a company has come, the more each incident costs it. Building a system that anticipates risk beats reacting to it after the fact.

  • External risk: theft of product and of intellectual property
  • Internal risk: financial schemes, blackmail of management, threats to staff
  • Audit of vulnerabilities in business processes
How candidate screening works

A decision on a candidate usually drags on for months: HR weighs the professional side, the security team checks whether the information holds up, and every stage costs money. Our system cuts both the time and the cost, and stays within the law.

  1. 01

    Token created

    The client enters the candidate in the system and receives a personal link to the questionnaire.

  2. 02

    The candidate fills it in

    The candidate enters the data himself, with our technical support on hand throughout.

  3. 03

    Automatic processing

    The system processes the form and compiles the primary data for the specialists.

  4. 04

    In-depth analysis

    Verification of the information, the business connections and the personal history, with further data added where needed.

  5. 05

    Report and risk level

    The candidate is placed on one of the risk levels for the company and the client receives the finished report.

Intelligence and analysis

Country and regional risk analysis

Every territory carries its own set of circumstances that bear on how business is done. They are worth assessing before entry rather than after.

  • National and religious character of the environment
  • Political risk and its effect on the business climate
  • Crime levels in the region and in the sector
  • Corruption levels and the lawful ways of dealing with state bodies
  • The terrorist threat in the region
Market and sector analysis

Entering a new industry calls for the whole picture: market dynamics, the balance of power between the players, where each is strong and weak. The depth is set by the scale of operations planned, from a broad survey to one narrow question.

Threat monitoring

The safety of an operation depends on how clean its information flow is. That flow is distorted both by hidden parties probing for weak points and by staff acting in bad faith.

  • Open-source collection and content analysis
  • Reputational enquiries and interviews
  • Measures to reduce the risks identified
Asset tracing and locating people

Finding individuals and their assets across Russia, the CIS and Eastern Europe: open-data analysis backed by field enquiries on the ground.

  • Identification of financial and physical assets
  • Locating fraud victims, heirs and family members
  • Corporate assets and hidden connections in insolvency
  • Building the case in court and recovering debt
Insurance claim investigation

According to the Association of European Investigators, roughly one insurance claim in ten turns out to be fraudulent.

  • Assessment of the claims submitted
  • Verification of the documents provided
  • Field investigation at the scene
Structured analysis

A toolkit for the cases where simpler approaches produce nothing: large volumes of data, tangled connections, competing versions of events.

  • Data decomposition and visualisation
  • Link and schematic analysis
  • Document review and analysis
  • Threat and risk analysis
  • Building and testing hypotheses
  • Analytical conflict management
