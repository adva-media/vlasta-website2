What we do

Brand protection and business security services

Trademark protection against counterfeiting, corporate, personal and integrated security. Four interconnected practice areas within a single methodology: risk analysis, threat prevention and client support all the way to court.

01 · Intellectual property 02 · Corporate security 03 · Personal security 04 · Integrated security
01

Intellectual property protection and anti-counterfeiting

End-to-end protection of trademarks, patents and other intangible assets of Western and Russian rights holders — from securing rights to shutting down the illegal production and trade of counterfeit goods in Russia and the EAEU.

  • Protection of trademarks, patents and copyright
  • TROIS customs recordation and work with the Federal Customs Service (FCS)
  • Detecting and dismantling counterfeit production, anti-counterfeiting raids
  • Combating illegal trade online (marketplaces, websites) and offline
  • Supporting rights holders in litigation
02

Corporate security

Protecting the company from internal and external threats: dishonest employees and counterparties, theft, fraud and corporate conflicts.

  • Background checks on personnel and job candidates
  • Counterparty vetting and due diligence
  • Investigations into internal theft and corporate fraud
  • Resolution of corporate disputes and conflicts
  • Vulnerability audits of business processes
03

Personal security

Confidential protection of the interests of owners and company principals: personal risk assessment and vetting of the business environment.

  • Personal and reputational risk assessment
  • Vetting of partners and business associates
  • Confidential transaction support
  • Protection of private interests in disputes
04

Integrated business security

Building a complete economic security system for the enterprise — a single framework uniting all areas of protection under continuous support and analytical intelligence.

  • Audit of the existing security system and vulnerabilities
  • Design and implementation of an economic security system
  • Ongoing support and threat monitoring
  • Analytical support for management decisions
