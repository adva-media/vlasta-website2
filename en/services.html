Brand protection and business security services

One methodology: risk analysis, threat prevention and support all the way to representing your interests in court — across Russia, the CIS and the EAEU.

01

Intelligence and analysis

We combine analysis with access to verified sources across Russia, the CIS and the former Eastern Bloc, helping build strategies that reduce risk before you enter a deal or a new market.

Business intelligence and market analysis

Risk analysis by region, country and sector before entering new markets, assessment of the competitive environment, sourcing and screening of counterparties, tracing of assets and business connections.

Expertise and valuation

Independent expertise in M&A, assessment of deal and project risk, asset tracing in insolvency, insurance claim investigation and structured analysis of complex situations.

02

Business security

Twenty years of experience have made us a team clients trust with the protection of their business and their principals. The single point of contact is reinforced by information screening and a full risk assessment of the sites involved.

Information and economic security

Background checks on staff and candidates, compliance to KYC / AML and FCPA / UKBA standards, identification of politically exposed persons and sanctions risk, audit of process vulnerabilities, investigation of internal theft and corporate fraud.

Physical security

Close protection for principals and guests, transport security from Meet & Greet through to multi-day details, and security for public and large-scale events. Every engagement has English-speaking coordinators available 24/7.

03

Brand and IP protection

In Russia we were among the very first to design and launch a trademark protection programme, back in 2006. We act on every type of infringement — from identity and confusing similarity through to unfair competition.

Intellectual property protection

Registration and maintenance of trademarks in Russia and abroad, entry into Russian and foreign customs registers, raids with police and customs, independent expert examination of seized goods, supervision of counterfeit destruction, civil claims and recovery of compensation.

Action against unfair competition

Online Brand Protection System: monitoring of search engines, social networks, marketplaces, messengers, app stores and classified-ad boards, digital fingerprint matching, pre-action measures with rapid delisting of listings and links, and action against parallel imports.

04

Consulting

We support the client from the initial brief through to representation in court, including engagement with state authorities and crisis response in contested situations.

Strategic consulting

Building a coherent economic security system for the enterprise, engagement with state and supervisory authorities, analytical support for management decisions, and crisis response.

Legal consulting

Pre-action cease and desist letters, preparation and filing of claims, appeals and enforcement of judgments, resolution of corporate disputes, and action against blackmail, greenmail and manipulation of reporting.
