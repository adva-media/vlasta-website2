From our practice

Case studies: anti-counterfeiting and corporate investigations

Real projects in trademark protection, anti-counterfeiting raids, counterparty vetting and internal investigations. Details have been changed or anonymised to preserve client confidentiality.

01
Corporate security

Employee screening for international companies in Russia

We run ongoing background checks on employees and candidates for the Russian offices of foreign companies — from routine personnel screening to in-depth vetting of top managers before appointment.

02
Corporate investigation

Investigating internal theft worth 10 million rubles

A medical device manufacturer discovered warehouse theft of goods worth around 10 million rubles. Our investigation uncovered collusion between employees at different levels; the scheme was stopped and assets recovered.

03
Anti-counterfeiting

Joint operation with customs and police against fake food products

Coordinated action with customs and law enforcement exposed a counterfeit supply channel: batches of fake packaging were seized and the illegal trade in products under the client's brand was stopped.

04
Online & offline brand protection

Comprehensive anti-counterfeiting programme for an automotive group

Multi-channel trademark protection for an automotive client: test purchases in retail, warehouse inspections, raids on points of sale and takedowns of websites and marketplace listings run by unauthorised sellers.

05
Anti-counterfeiting raid

Raid on an illegal factory producing fake auto parts

A supplier of truck components found high-quality fakes on the market amid falling revenue. Our specialists traced the counterfeit production source; the facility was neutralised.

06
Due diligence

Vetting a business partner in a CIS country

Due diligence on a prospective partner in a post-Soviet state with limited official records. The check exposed a fraudster who had previously caused significant losses to other investors.

07
Litigation support

Building the evidence base in an international theft case

A multi-country theft case worth millions of euros: official interstate cooperation was blocked, yet our team assembled an evidence base that secured the client's interests in court.

08
Business intelligence

Structured assessment of a senior executive's position

Assessing the shifting business standing of a high-ranking executive through hypothesis testing: a combination of open sources (OSINT) and field intelligence gave the client a solid basis for a decision.

09
Corporate disputes

Resolving a corporate conflict and supplier collusion

Internal collusion between the client's employees and suppliers was uncovered and resolved. The company continued operating without litigation costs or reputational damage.
